Turn any YouTube song into karaoke

AI strips the vocals, synced lyrics fill as they're sung, and everyone queues songs from their phone. Pronunciation guides for Chinese, Cantonese, Korean and Japanese.

A KTV room, in your living room

Everything a karaoke box does, built from songs you already know.

Any song on YouTube

Search from your phone. KTVibes downloads the song and its music video, and skips reactions, dance practices, mixes and karaoke uploads.

♫

AI vocal removal

Demucs separates the singer from the music, on an NVIDIA GPU, Apple Silicon or CPU. Keep a little guide vocal if you like.

Aa

Lyrics that fill as they're sung

Synced lyrics from LRCLIB, timed character by character against the vocals by forced alignment.

♯

Key and speed

Shift ±6 semitones without changing tempo, or slow a song down without changing pitch.

Phone remote

Scan the QR code on the TV to join. One shared queue for everyone, with undo.

Sing in any language

Install in one line

Needs a computer connected to the TV. An NVIDIA GPU or Apple Silicon Mac makes songs ready in seconds.

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/andyleenz/KTVibes/main/install.sh | bash
  1. Run ktvibes
  2. Open http://localhost:8765/tv on the TV and click Enable sound
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone and start queuing
