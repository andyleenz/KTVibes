Install in one command

Needs a computer connected to the TV. An NVIDIA GPU or Apple Silicon Mac makes songs ready in seconds.

  1. Run ktvibes
  2. Open http://localhost:8765/tv on the TV and click Enable sound
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone and start queuing

Requirements & FAQ

System

Linux, macOS or Windows (the Windows installer is new and untested). Needs git, ffmpeg and Node.js 22+; uv installs Python 3.12.

GPU

NVIDIA (CUDA 12.8 driver) or Apple Silicon. CPU works, but is much slower.

Speed

Vocal separation took 5.3 s for a 4:45 song on an RTX 5070 (model warm) and 33.6 s for a 6:00 song on an M1 Pro. Word alignment takes 1–5 s per song.

Disk

About 8 GB: the install is 6.5 GB (mostly PyTorch), plus an ~80 MB separation model and ~1.2 GB alignment model on first use. Each cached song is about 30 MB.

Docker

Not yet. It's on the roadmap.

Is it legal?

KTVibes downloads from YouTube with yt-dlp to your own machine, for personal at-home use. Respect artists' rights and YouTube's terms where you live. It ships no music.

Does it need the internet?

Yes, to search, download songs and fetch lyrics. Cached songs play from disk.

Who can control it?

Anyone on the same local network who scans the QR code. It isn't meant to be exposed to the internet.

How is it different from PiKaraoke, Karaoke Eternal or UltraStar?

AI vocal removal on any YouTube song, with an adjustable guide-vocal slider; a character-by-character KTV wipe timed to the vocals; and pinyin, jyutping, romaji and Hangul guides.

Where do lyrics come from?

LRCLIB, a community-run lyrics database. Some songs have none. You can pick another language version from the remote.
