KTVibes downloads FFmpeg and FFprobe on first launch from Martin Riedl's FFmpeg build server:
https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/

The Apple silicon release builds are compiled from FFmpeg and include third-party codec and
library components. Build configuration, source links, and licensing information are published
by the build maintainer:
https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

The FFmpeg executables are downloaded directly to the user's Application Support/KTVibes/tools
directory and are not included in the KTVibes application bundle or disk image.
